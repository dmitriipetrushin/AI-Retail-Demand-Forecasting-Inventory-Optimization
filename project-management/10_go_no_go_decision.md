# GO / NO-GO decision

## Local portfolio demonstration: GO

Real data processed: 541,909 raw / 520,893 retained records, 25 selected products. Baselines and candidates ran. Chronology and initial holdout are hash-verified. Development improvement is 16.94% against a fixed 5% target. Simulation constraints pass for all nine strategy/scenario runs. Automated test evidence: {'tests': 55, 'failures': 0, 'errors': 0, 'skipped': 0}. Browser evidence: passed; see reports/browser-verification.json.

## Controlled business analytical pilot: CONDITIONAL GO

The technical hypothesis warrants a shadow-mode study, conditional on validated purchasing prices, stock records, supplier lead times, budget authority, demand availability/censoring, a named operational owner and fictional-role approvals becoming real organizational decisions. Use human review and baseline fallback. No autonomous procurement.

## Production inventory deployment: NO-GO

No stock truth, live supplier integration, multi-year history, calibrated uncertainty, SLOs, role-based authorization, durable audit trail or drift/incident response integration has been validated. A working portfolio app does not satisfy these requirements.

Development forecast target passed, but base-scenario forecast/optimized policies cost more than the traditional baseline and service is weak. Observed sales are censored demand proxies and operational costs/stock are assumed. Retain the baseline for purchasing; consider only a conditional analytical shadow pilot with real operational inputs and monitoring. Production purchasing is not supported.

Decision criteria were specified before the final holdout. Readiness is generated from current evidence; it is not an assertion that any business sponsor approved a deployment. Scope: one independent project, not an actual company rollout.
